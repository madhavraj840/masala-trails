Our story

Two floors,
one long evening.

Downstairs is the family restaurant — teal booths, walnut tables, room for the whole table to argue over the last piece of naan. Upstairs is the rooftop lounge, where the evening slows down and the glasses get taller. Same kitchen for both: North Indian that tastes like somebody's home, Chinese cooked hard and fast, and Italian that isn't an afterthought.
